LTCG 8-byte stack-alignment placement (push ebp; mov ebp,esp; and esp,-8), c2.dll 8460473c..., 10-28
==================================================================================================
STATUS (10-31): decision code LOCATED, c2 0x1070cd11; model + simulator in inl_notes.txt Alignment pass (agent 10-31).

KEY RESULT (PINNED, oracle relink == normal link)
  The placement is NOT decided from the callee's own inputs at its compile:
  - compile order (F lines) of all chain fns is identical between a build where Com_ShutdownInternal
    hoists (ours) and one where it self-aligns (retail shape);
  - the callee's sym (first 0x100 bytes, dumped at compile start by cave cv/mid.s) is identical too;
  - its inline decisions (I/V lines) are identical.
  So it is a whole-program pre-pass (before codegen), driven by the call graph / call-site counts.

TRIGGER FOUND (PINNED, ox_v*.exe in lab/w200/alw)
  Com_ShutdownInternal self-aligns (retail) and Com_Shutdown / SV_KillServer_f lose their align
  (retail) when UI_Init (ui_main_mp.cpp ~3118) drops its hand-inlined copies of:
    RegisterBool("ui_multiplayer") block  (direct Dvar_Reregister + Dvar_RegisterNew sites)
    AND one direct Dvar_SetVariant site   (either the SetBoolByName or the SetString block)
  Bisect (mkv.py mask: 1 GetFloat, 2 SetBoolByName, 4 SetString, 8 RegisterBool):
    flips: 10 12 11 13 14 15      no flip: 1 2 4 8 3 5 6 9 7
  Whole-link static site counts ([sym+0x69], V col 7) separate them exactly:
    flip  <=> Dvar_RegisterNew 2->1 AND Dvar_Reregister 2->1 AND Dvar_SetVariant 18->17.
  These callees reach Com_Error -> Com_ErrorCleanup -> Com_ShutdownInternal, so the pass looks
  at site counts / alignment state of callers several levels away, not just direct callers.
  UI_Init is compiled AFTER Com_ShutdownInternal and has no edge to it, which again rules out
  any compile-time (bottom-up) decision.

HYPOTHESIS (not proven): a pre-pass marks "entry already 8-aligned" per fn from its callers and
  hoists `and esp` into callers when that is cheaper by static site count; the Dvar_* site counts
  change which callers count as aligned on the Com_Error path, which flips the cheaper choice for
  Com_ShutdownInternal.
