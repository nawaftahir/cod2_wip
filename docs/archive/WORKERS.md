is worktrees/gemini, branch workers/gemini-gfx, base871d13e. Its entry point is
[GEMINI_START_HERE.md](worktrees/gemini/GEMINI_START_HERE.md), with a pinned
MISSION.json and baseline scene source hash. SCENE_01 owns five scene-command
bodies/1363 target bytes; native tests first, serialized linked acceptance here.

layout/retail_functions.tsv and layout/tu_work_queue.tsv under target/work/
record the inventory, supported/unknown owners and heuristic complexity. Refresh
with `python3 target/work/tools/work_queue.py` after file_layout.py and a fresh
completed build. work_queue.json pins the inputs. Known emitted owners are not
proof of the original PC TU boundaries, and unknowns are not assigned by guess.

Codex will not edit it during the mission. Further workers require separate
worktrees, disjoint TU claims and their own pinned missions; do not share this
checkout between simultaneous writers. Never count a worker's self-reported match.

inputs; no extra DLL build or retail file was copied. Primary uncommitted work
and unrelated staged files remain in the primary checkout. The worker starts
from the accepted commit, not those unreviewed drafts. Native test success is
not linked byte/binding closure. Integration is one mission at a time, all prior
extents retained; whole-file SHA and GPU smoke remain final unfinished gates.
