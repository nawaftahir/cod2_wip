# Historical renderer research

Read-only input snapshots from the old renderer-progress, codex/renderer and
glm archives. No files here are promoted to verified source or counted toward
the active renderer percentage. Use gfx/main and its gfx worktrees for progress.

workspace/research/renderer-progress preserves its original one-commit saved
source/evidence history and hash manifest. The source/evidence snapshots for
codex-renderer and glm are inventoried in data/archive_manifest.json.
Generated binaries, SDK/CRT payloads, crash dumps, caches and Ghidra projects
are excluded. Original local archives remain untouched.

The old saved JPEG/renderer numbers are historical claims with their original
scope, not additions to the current C/C++ meter. Re-run the active renderer's
byte AND binding gates before accepting any recovered function or complete TU.
